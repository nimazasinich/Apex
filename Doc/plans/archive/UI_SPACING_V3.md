# APEX UI spacing correction — v3

This pass intentionally removes the compressed treatment.

## Rules used

- More content is shown through scrolling, not by squeezing cards and rows.
- Core spacing scale: 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, and 30 pixels.
- Standard panels: 20 px internal padding.
- Metric cards: 18–20 px internal padding and at least 132 px height on desktop.
- Tables: 13–15 px header padding and 14–15 px cell padding.
- Order fields: at least 42 px high with 13 px vertical form gaps.
- Watchlist rows: at least 48 px high.
- Market cards: at least 172 px wide and horizontally scrollable.
- Crypto icons remain modest in size, but their surrounding card/row spacing is increased.
